Dice Dash

Fill 1–12 before your rival

Dice Dash is a quick, easy-to-learn dice race for one or two players. Roll six dice, keep the matches, and be the first to fill every number from 1 to 12.

Play a friend on one phone, or take on the Easy, Normal or Hard bot — even offline.
